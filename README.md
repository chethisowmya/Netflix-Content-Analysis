# 🎬 Netflix Content Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Netflix is one of the world's leading streaming platforms, offering thousands of movies and TV shows across multiple countries and genres. Understanding trends in Netflix's content library can provide valuable insights into its content strategy, audience preferences, and global expansion.

This project performs an **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the Netflix Titles dataset to uncover meaningful patterns related to content type, release trends, genres, countries, ratings, and duration. The findings are translated into business insights and actionable recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

As Netflix continues to expand its global content library, understanding historical trends in content production and distribution is essential for making strategic business decisions.

This analysis aims to answer questions such as:

- How has Netflix's content library evolved over time?
- What proportion of the catalog consists of Movies versus TV Shows?
- Which countries contribute the most content?
- Which ratings are most common?
- What genres dominate the platform?
- What business insights can be derived from these patterns?

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand the structure and quality of the dataset.
- Clean and preprocess the data.
- Perform exploratory data analysis using statistical summaries and visualizations.
- Analyze trends across content type, release year, country, rating, duration, and genres.
- Identify key business insights.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations.

---

## 📂 Dataset

This project uses a dataset named **`netflix.csv`**, which contains information about Netflix movies and TV shows, including title, type, director, cast, country, release year, rating, duration, genres, and description.

The dataset is included in this repository to enable reproduction of the analysis presented in this project.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Some of the major insights obtained include:

- Movies make up a significantly larger portion of Netflix's catalog than TV Shows.
- Netflix experienced rapid content growth after 2015.
- The United States contributes the highest number of titles.
- India is one of the largest contributors to Netflix's content library.
- International Movies represent one of the largest content categories.
- Most movies have durations between 80 and 120 minutes.
- TV-MA is among the most common audience ratings, indicating a strong focus on mature audiences.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are suggested:

- Continue expanding international content to support global audience growth.
- Invest further in regions with increasing content production.
- Maintain a balanced mix of Movies and TV Shows.
- Strengthen investments in popular genres with consistently high audience demand.
- Use historical release trends to optimize future content acquisition and production strategies.
